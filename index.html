Before you come in

Questions, answered
without the jargon.

Will dental treatment hurt?

Your dentist will discuss suitable ways to keep you comfortable, including local anaesthetic when appropriate. Tell the team what worries you and agree a pause signal before treatment.

Can I spread the cost?

Ask the clinic about current payment plans, finance eligibility and insurance arrangements. Provider terms, interest, eligibility and total payable should be explained before you agree.

Can I change or cancel my appointment?

If your plans change, please contact the clinic as early as possible so we can help arrange another suitable time.

What if I haven’t seen a dentist in years?

You can start again at any time. You’ll be treated with respect, not judgement, and the team can help you make a plan that feels manageable.
